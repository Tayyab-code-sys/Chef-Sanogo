Professional Profile

Over 20 years in professional kitchens.

Brahema Sanogo is a Corporate Chef at Royal Private Affairs in Riyadh. Since 2006 he has worked in leading kitchens across Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Israel, holding roles from Commis to Head Chef. Trained in French cuisine and qualified in nutrition, he has extensive knowledge of Italian, European, Mediterranean, and Arabic cuisine, and experience in menu development, healthy dishes, and the management of professional kitchen teams and formal functions.
